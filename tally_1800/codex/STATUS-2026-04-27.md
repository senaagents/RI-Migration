## Latest Sales Breakthrough

Lane A productized a strict page-crossing compact Sales reader:

```text
source: sales_logical_compact
direct source rows: 1,148
source rows matching Rosetta: 1,128
source-local extras: 20
final 61 Sales table: 2,051 / 2,068 exact full tuples, 83 extras
hard XML repair rows after this pass: 64
```

New row rules:

- Inline compact row:
  `stock -> ledger/stock-repeat -> stock-repeat -> unit -> amount -> qty -> rate`.
- Rows without inline ledger resolve only through unique field-coded
  ledger/amount pairs in the same voucher tree.
- Some rows store stock+rate first, then a following
  `00 50 f6 01 00 10 <len>` block stores amount and quantity.
- Page-split values can resume after a 28-byte next-page header.

Rejected companion rule:

```text
sales_logical_post_rate_f6 broad promotion
  recovers at least one real miss, but adds Rosetta extras 31956 and 55157
```

The accepted rule remains narrow: it is for the already-proven compact Sales
row shape when the row crosses a page boundary. It is not a license to promote
all logical F6 page-split rows.

Rules now proved enough for typed rows:

- `stock + rate` header followed by
  `00 50 f6 01 00 10 <len>` where `0x0002/0009` is amount and
  `0x0002/000b` is quantity.
- Strict columnar rows:
  `<flags> f6 01 44 00 <stock_id>` for amount-like values and
  `<flags> f6 01 46 00 <stock_id>` for quantity-like values.
- For `0x04` rows, pair amount `+24` with quantity `+32`, and amount `+32`
  with quantity `+24`; derive rate as `abs(amount) / abs(quantity)`.
- A compact field marker can end at byte `511`; the value resumes at offset
  `0x1c` on the next page. `repair_split_i64()` handles this marker-edge split
  in addition to i64 payload splits.
- `scripts/probe_journal_inventory_rows.py` now uses a logical F6 block reader
  that reconstructs the `00 50 f6 01 00 10 <len>` header and body through page
  continuation bytes.

Largest remaining misses after the repair are real layout variants or
association gaps, not the old marker-edge corruption. Examples:

## Purchase Monthly Finding

`31-Puchase(Monthly)` is the next major inventory family:

```text
truth_rows=7682
Journal probe union exact item/qty/rate/amount=7667/7682
Hybrid purchase exact with ledger=7645/7682
```

Rosetta shows all inventory rows in a given `31-Puchase(Monthly)` voucher share
one inventory ledger, but the raw bytes still expose safer row-level association
through ledger/amount blocks.

Needed parser is a hybrid:

- Stock row anchor:
  `02 00 00 03 <stock_id>`.
- Then either:
  - `68 00 00 03 <same stock_id>` followed by
    `6d 00 00 03 <same stock_id>`, with ledger resolved from the nearest
    preceding `0x0002/0003 <ledger_id> ... 0x0002/0009 <same amount>` block.
  - or `69 00 00 03 <ledger_id>` followed by
    `6e 00 00 03 <ledger_id>` for direct inline ledger rows.
- Rate from nearby `0x0002/000c`.
- Amount and quantity from following
  `00 50 f6 01 00 10 <len>` sub-record using `0x0002/0009` and `0x0002/000b`.
- F6 parsing must tolerate page-split continuation where the F6 header starts
  near a page end and amount/quantity markers occur after next-page header/noise.
- A new stock-rate-F6 subfamily is now promoted:
  `02 00 00 03 <stock_id>`, `02 00 00 0c <rate>`, nearby unit id, then
  `00 50 f6 01 00 10 <len>` containing `0x0002/0009` negative amount and
  `0x0002/000b` quantity. Ledger resolution uses the closest preceding signed
  allocation carrying the same negative amount.
- Synthetic `PURCHASE_XML001` proves one controlled row:
  voucher `11`, item `211`, ledger `214`, unit `210`, qty `4.00`, rate
  `55.55`, amount `-222.20`.
- Replaying on Avinash `070525` emits `75` `purchase_stock_rate_f6` rows under
  `31-Puchase(Monthly)`; all `75` match Rosetta exact full tuples and add
  `0` extras. Hard XML repair vouchers drop `84 -> 65`, specifically
